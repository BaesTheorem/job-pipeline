# Capability Boundaries (verb level)

Gate 4 scores each JD line against this table (see docs/fit-scorecard.md).
Copy to profile/capabilities.md (gitignored) and replace every row with your own.

Levels, highest first: **Own/Build > Administer > Operate > Support > Exposure > None**

- Own/Build: you designed, deployed or built it, and you were accountable for it.
- Administer: you configured and maintained it for others.
- Operate: you used it as a regular part of the work.
- Support: you helped users who used it, or you fixed problems with it for them.
- Exposure: you know what it is and have touched it, but not as part of the work.

Be strict. A row that is one level too high costs you interviews where you
cannot answer the follow-up questions. Mark an area you have not confirmed as
"not confirmed"; the scorecard then counts it as Partial at most.
